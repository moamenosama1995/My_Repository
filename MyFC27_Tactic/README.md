## 📋 Player Roles & Instructions

### 🎯 Attackers
* **Rodrygo (ST - Left):** `False 9 +` (Attack) - Drops deep to link play, dragging center-backs out of position.
* **Mbappé (ST - Right):** `Advanced Forward ++` (Attack) - Pushes the defensive line and acts as the primary clinical goalscorer.
* **Vini Jr. (LW):** `Inside Forward ++` (Attack) - Cuts inside dynamically from the left wing to create overload situations.
* **Diomande (RW):** `Inside Forward +` (Balanced) - Provides structured width and late back-post runs.

### ⚙️ Midfield Control
* **Bellingham (CM - Left):** `Box-To-Box ++` (Ball-Winning) - Covers the entire pitch, transitioning heavily between defense and attack.
* **Tchouaméni (CM - Right):** `Holding +` (Defend) - Sits in front of the defense to break up counter-attacks and secure the central pivot.

### 🛡️ Defense & Goalkeeper
* **Cucurella (LB):** `Falseback +` (Defend) - Inverts into the midfield during possession to assist build-up and prevent counter-attacks.
* **Valverde (RB):** `Attacking Wingback +` (Attack) - Explodes forward down the right flank to overlap and provide crosses.
* **Álvaro Carreras& Éder Militão (CBs):** `Ball-Playing Block` (Defend) - Safe distribution from the back line.
* **Courtois (GK):** `Goalkeeper ++` (Balanced) - Elite traditional shot-stopping.

## 📊 Tactical Pros & Cons

### 🟢 Pros
1. **Lethal Counter-Attacks:** The 4-man frontline creates constant 1v1 situations against opponent defenders.
2. **Midfield Inversion:** The `Falseback +` role allows the team to match 3-man midfields during build-up.
3. **Overlapping Right Flank:** Exploits space heavily on the right wing with Valverde pushing high up.

### 🔴 Cons
1. **Vulnerable to Counters:** If the right-back is caught out of position, the right flank can be exposed.
2. **High Stamina Demand:** The Box-to-Box midfielder requires high stamina stats to maintain influence all game.
